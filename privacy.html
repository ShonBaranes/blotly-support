Blotly

Privacy Policy · effective 4 October 2026

Support Privacy Policy

Blotly (“the app”) is made by Nissim Shon Baranes. It helps you find and hide private details — faces, numbers, addresses, codes and more — in photos, screenshots, documents and videos before you share them.

The short version

  • Your photos, videos and documents are checked and protected on your device. The app has no servers and makes no network requests of its own.
  • The app does not collect, upload, sell or share your media or any information about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Anything leaves your device only when you save or share it, to a place you choose.
  • Links to websites open in your browser only when you tap them.

What the app does with your media

When you bring a photo, screenshot, PDF or video into the app, it is analysed on your device with Apple’s built-in frameworks (for example, Vision for faces, text and codes) and with rules that run inside the app. Text recognised in your media is used only to find sensitive details on screen; it is not stored and not sent anywhere.

Protection (blur, pixelate, solid, emoji) is drawn into a new copy. Your original is never changed. Exported images are rewritten without location, camera and other metadata; exported PDFs are rebuilt as images without a text layer.

Like other app data on your iPhone or iPad, settings, presets and Projects are included in your device backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you use backups. Files waiting to be opened from other apps are excluded from backups and are unreadable while your device is locked.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to save or share an export — for example to your photo library, the Files app, or another app through the share sheet. What happens there is governed by that service.

Ratings

After you have saved or shared protected files a few times, the app may ask iOS to show Apple’s standard rating request. Whether it appears, and anything you enter, is handled by Apple; the app doesn’t see your answer.

Permissions

All permissions are optional and can be changed in iOS Settings at any time.

  • Photos (add only): to save protected copies to your library. With this permission the app cannot see your other photos.
  • Photos (read): only if you use the Shortcuts action that opens your most recent screenshot.
  • Camera: only if you take a photo or scan a document in the app.
  • Clipboard: read only when you tap the Paste button.

Sharing from other apps

When you share photos, PDFs or videos to Blotly from another app, its share extension copies them into a private folder that only the app can read and asks you to open the app. They open the next time you open the app and are deleted from that folder as soon as they have opened. If you cancel the share, nothing is kept.

Shortcuts

The app’s Shortcuts actions run on your device and return the protected file to the Shortcuts app. Nothing is sent anywhere by the app.

Links to other websites

Home and Settings link to Classeem, our learning website, and Settings links to this support site. They open in your browser only when you tap them. The Classeem link includes campaign labels that tell the website the visit came from this app; they do not identify you. Websites you open have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Children

The app is a general-audience tool. It does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your media is processed in memory on your device. Files waiting to be opened from other apps are protected with iOS data protection while your device is locked. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on the device.
