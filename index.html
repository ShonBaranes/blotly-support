Support Privacy Policy

Blotly finds private details in photos, screenshots, documents and videos — faces, phone numbers, addresses, card numbers, codes and more — and hides them before you share. You review every finding, and the protected copy is checked again before you save or share it.

Everything is processed on your device. No account, no sign-up, no cloud upload, no analytics and no tracking.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and feedback are all welcome — please email shon13b@gmail.com.

It helps to include your device model (for example iPhone 16 or iPad Air), your iOS version, and a short description of what you expected to happen. Screenshots are very useful for anything visual — please blur anything private first.

Getting started

  1. Tap Protect and choose a source: Photos & videos, Files, Scan, Camera or Paste.
  2. Review what was found. Switch any finding on or off, or draw over anything the scan missed.
  3. Tap Export. Blotly protects a new copy, removes metadata, and checks the result again before you save or share it.

Sharing from another app

In Photos, Files or another app, tap Share → Blotly. You’ll see “Saved for Blotly” — tap Done, then open Blotly; what you shared opens straight away. iOS doesn’t let share extensions open their app automatically, so it waits for you, and it stays waiting even if you restart your device. If you were already editing something, the shared item opens when you close it.

Common questions

Something wasn’t detected

Detection is a first pass — always look before you share. Known limits:

  • Very small or distant faces can be missed.
  • Personal names aren’t recognised on their own. Draw a box over them, or use the Maximum Privacy preset, which protects all text.
  • Licence plates are assisted: small, angled or dirty plates can be missed.
  • Hebrew text isn’t read (Apple’s text recognition doesn’t support it yet). Numbers, emails and links on their own lines still are.

Use the box or brush tool for anything the scan missed.

What does “Not verified” mean?

After an export, Blotly checks the exported file again. If that check couldn’t run to the end, it says Not verified instead of claiming it’s clean — look the file over before sharing. Still detectable means a protected area (or, for a PDF, its text) can still be read: use a stronger style or Solid and export again.

Videos

Faces are followed through the clip. If someone disappears for more than about two seconds, Blotly treats that as them leaving the frame — check the timeline and draw a box for any stretch you want covered. The check after export samples frames from the exported video, so play it through before sharing.

Documents

Exported PDFs are rebuilt as images, so no hidden text remains. Use Solid for documents; blur and pixelate hide detail but don’t remove it.

Shortcuts

The Shortcuts actions return a protected copy straight to Shortcuts. They don’t run the check after export, so look at the result before sharing it.

Where are my projects?

In Projects, on this device only. They hold the protected result (never the original). Delete one with its menu, or use Clear all projects in Projects or Settings.

Does anything get uploaded?

No. There’s no account and no cloud. Blotly makes no network requests of its own. Links to websites open in your browser only when you tap them.

Permissions

Photos (add only) to save copies; Photos (read) only for the “latest screenshot” Shortcut; Camera only when you use it. Change them any time in iOS Settings → Blotly.

How do I delete my data?

Delete projects in Projects, or delete the app to remove everything Blotly has stored. There is no server-side copy to request removal of.

Privacy

Blotly does not collect personal data. The full policy is on the Privacy Policy page.
